# ELEVEN 11⭐

An engaging iOS task management and positive reinforcement application designed for children and parents, featuring gamified rewards, Siri integration, smart notifications, and AI-powered recommendations.


---

## 👥 Team
Designed and engineered by **Team 11** at the **Apple Developer Academy @ Tuwaiq**.

---

## 💡 Overview
**ELEVEN** turns daily routines and household chores into a rewarding journey for children. Parents assign custom tasks, while children earn points upon completion to redeem parent-approved rewards. With voice shortcuts via Siri, timely smart notifications, and intelligent AI recommendations, the app fosters independence and positive behavioral habits in an intuitive, child-friendly interface.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
* 📋 **Parent-Managed Tasks:** Parents can create, schedule, and assign daily activities or chores with assigned point values.
* 🎁 **Custom Reward Shop:** Children collect earned points to redeem tangible rewards defined and approved by parents.
* 🤖 **AI Task & Reward Recommendations:** Smart suggestion engine that analyzes progress and recommends age-appropriate tasks, optimal point distributions, and tailored incentives.
* 🎙️ **Siri Shortcuts Integration:** Hands-free interaction allowing children and parents to check tasks and log completions using Siri voice commands.
* 🔔 **Interactive Notifications:** Contextual reminders and celebratory milestone alerts to keep kids motivated.
* 🦅 **Engaging UI/UX:** Clean, colorful, and accessible interface following Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (HIG).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Apple Frameworks
* **Language:** Swift
* **UI Framework:** SwiftUI
* **Voice & Shortcuts:** SiriKit / App Intents Framework
* **Intelligence & Logic:** Core ML / Foundation Models for AI-driven task and reward recommendations
* **Notifications:** UserNotifications Framework
* **IDE:** Xcode
